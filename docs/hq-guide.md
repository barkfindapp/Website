# HQ guide

Master copy: the Claude doc "HQ guide for Megan". Last updated 19 October 2026.

## Getting in and what you can see

HQ is at barkfind.com/hq. Sign in with your own account and the code from your authenticator app. On your phone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and it behaves like an app.

You see the parts of HQ your permissions allow. For Megan, today, that is:

- **Outreach:** venues, press, creator prospects and community contacts. You can view, add, edit, log and draft. You cannot delete; ask Josh.
- **Creators:** applications, active creators, their codes and posts waiting for approval.
- **Ask Ralphy:** questions about anything you can see.

Money, payouts and renewals are hidden from you, and Ralphy leaves them out too. If you need a tab you cannot see, ask Josh to add it in Admin, Team.

## The Outreach tab

Start each session on **Follow up due**: anyone whose next follow-up date is today or earlier. Then work **To contact**.

| View | Who is in it |
| --- | --- |
| To contact | Not contacted yet |
| Follow up due | Next follow-up date is today or earlier |
| Waiting on them | Contacted, no reply yet |
| Warm | Replied, warm or agreed |
| Shared | Has posted, shared or displayed BarkFind, or is live |
| Declined and parked | Said no, or set aside for now |
| All | Everyone |

The filters narrow any view by kind, status, area, owner, tag, place category and minimum BarkFind rating. Search finds a name.

Each row shows the BarkFind rating for venues. A red **Open place report** flag means someone has reported a problem with that listing: don't contact the venue until it is fixed.

Statuses, in the order a contact usually moves:

1. **To contact:** on the list, nothing sent.
2. **Contacted:** you have sent something.
3. **Replied:** they wrote back.
4. **Warm:** interested, a conversation is going.
5. **Agreed:** they have said yes to something specific.
6. **Shared** or **Live:** they have posted, shared, put up a sticker, or are up and running.
7. **Declined** or **Parked:** a no, or not now.

## Working a contact

Tap a contact to open its panel. For a venue it shows the place: photo, category, BarkFind rating, review count, and **Open in Places**.

**Log every contact, including the ones outside HQ.** That is how Josh and you both know where things stand.

1. Under **Log a contact**, write what happened in a line, for example "DM'd on Instagram about their listing" or "Popped in, spoke to Sam, will reply next week".
2. Pick **How** (email, Instagram DM, phone, in person, other) and **Which way** (you to them, or them to you).
3. Set a new status if it changed, and the **Next follow-up** date.
4. Tap **Log it**.

**Draft a message with Claude** writes a first draft from the contact's record and history. Read every word before using it:

- It only knows what is in the record, so a vague next action gives a vague draft. Write next actions plainly, for example "Tell them they're already on BarkFind and can claim their listing free".
- It writes as Josh, in the first person. Change it to your own voice and name if you are sending it.
- Check the facts: ratings change as reviews come in. Open the place to see today's rating.

**Edit details** holds the name, contact details, area, owner, tags, handles and notes. **Linked place** connects a venue to its BarkFind listing; search by name or postcode, tap Link, then Save changes. A place already linked to another contact is refused, and Unlink removes a link.

## Adding contacts

**By hand:** use Add, pick the kind (venue, press, creator prospect, community, partner, other), fill in what you have, and set a next action and follow-up date. For a venue, give the area (for example Weston-super-Mare or BS23) so filters work.

**Import from places** brings in venues that are already on BarkFind:

1. Choose a category (pub, café and so on), an area, and a minimum BarkFind rating. The area matches the address, so a town name or a postcode start such as BS23 both work.
2. Check the count and the first twenty names it shows.
3. Tap again to import. It adds up to 200 at a time, best rated first, and skips any venue already in Outreach.

Start small: one area and a high minimum rating, so the list stays workable. A venue with an open place report should wait until the listing is fixed.

**Tags** group contacts across kinds, for example `weston-launch` or `sticker-sent`. Select several rows to tag them in one go; you get a few seconds to undo.

## Creators

Creators live in Act, Creators. Applications from the form on barkfind.com/creators arrive there, and active creators show their code and how many people have used it.

**Posts to approve come first.** Under the current terms, a creator's first two posts need an OK within 2 working days. They send drafts to info@barkfind.com. Check each one against the five points on the creators page:

- BarkFind on screen: the map, a real place and what Mylo says about it
- A real visit with their dog, filmed by them
- Their code on screen and in the caption
- "Ad" or "Paid partnership" at the start, visible without tapping "more"
- At least 20 seconds long

Reply with an OK or exactly what to change. After the first two, there is no approval step.

When someone you have been talking to applies, add a note on their Outreach contact saying they have applied, and set them to Agreed once they are approved, so the history stays together. Payment amounts and payouts stay with Josh.

## Asking Ralphy

Ralphy answers questions about what you can see in HQ, using today's live figures: who is due a follow-up, how a creator's code is doing, what is waiting in a queue. Good questions:

- "Who in outreach is overdue?"
- "How many people have used Chlo's code?"
- "Which venues in Weston have I not contacted yet?"

Ralphy knows who you are and only sees what your permissions allow. For "how do I" questions it answers from this guide, and says so when the guide does not cover something: then ask Josh.

Ralphy can be wrong. Check a figure in the tab before acting on it.

## The rules

1. **Log everything**, the same day, including DMs, calls and visits.
2. **Always set a next follow-up date**, or the contact drops out of Follow up due.
3. **Never contact a venue with an open place report.** Tell Josh instead.
4. **One message at a time, written for that person.** No mail merges, no copy-paste blasts.
5. **Only say what is true and checkable:** a rating you have just looked at, a listing that exists. No promises of features, coverage or money.
6. **Never offer anything for an App Store rating or review.** Apple does not allow it.
7. **If someone asks not to be contacted**, set them to Declined, note why, and never contact them again.
8. **UK English, no long dashes, no exclamation marks.** Write the way you talk.

Anything unclear, or a contact that wants to talk money, ads or partnerships: pass it to Josh.
